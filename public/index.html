A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Questions?
Start here.

Getting familiar with BBTrader.

What is BBTrader?+

BBTrader stands for Broker Bhai Trader. Explore the markets through a focused interface with current forex and crypto quotes and interactive charts.

Where do the prices come from?+

Forex and crypto quote cards use Kraken's public API, refreshed every 15 seconds. Indian indices use Yahoo Finance, checked every 60 seconds, with delayed or last-close labels. Each chart uses history from its matching provider, with the latest price taken from the same snapshot as its quote cards.

Do chart prices match the quote cards?+

Yes. For each instrument, the latest-price labels and current chart candle use the same provider snapshot. Hovering an older candle shows its historical price, while the latest-price label remains visible.

Can I place trades or deposit funds here?+

This page provides market information and chart exploration. Account opening, order execution, and deposits are not connected.

What happens if a feed is unavailable?+

Quote fields show an unavailable state instead of invented prices. The update label shows connection status. Charts show a history-unavailable message if the historical feed cannot load. No sample prices are substituted.
